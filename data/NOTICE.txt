The operator downloads and validates the fixed source outside Git.
The original source CSV is not committed or included in the prepared images.
Current published assets:
- Frozen public prepared splits from auphong2707/hospital-readmission-risk-data,
  revision 4a61a3591f079628c72f5edbc7494ca7dda8741d; no refitting or repartitioning.
- Target 1: readmitted <30; target 0: >30 or NO, according to the source labels.
- Already standardized 113-feature inputs. The incompatible 120-feature scaler is excluded.
- Restored original patient linkage for aggregate overlap checks; original missing
  race remains flagged separately from the public sidecar's Caucasian imputation.
- Shared model artifact from auphong2707/hospital-readmission-phase2-lr,
  revision e1f7fcc0f4f8e273fc56eb35018ec70bbccbee61, with its MIT source README.
  The checkpoint is conditionally registered for prepared-input educational use.
  Its image stores files only, not an executable inference runtime.
The prepared-data and model-artifact manifests record feature order, source
checksums, original-source hash, versions and known limitations. No new model
training, preprocessing fit or model prediction is performed by publication.

Known limitations: cross-split patient overlap (train/test 5086,
train/validation 5055, validation/test 1760), validation reused in model
development, test original race missing in 341 records, unresolved historical
113-feature preprocessing and source target-encoding provenance. These assets
do not establish a leakage-free independent holdout.

This is not a clinical system or evidence of clinical performance.
Source NO means no recorded readmission, not proof of unlimited follow-up.
The source does not provide per-feature timestamps; temporal leakage cannot
be certified from these assets alone. The dataset creators and UCI do not
endorse this workshop.
